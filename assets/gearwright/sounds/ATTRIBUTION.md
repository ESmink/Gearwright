# Sound attribution

## Current machine sounds

Pipe/nozzle water, airflow, bellows, flywheel, pump, transmission, work cues, and pressure creaks use original
Gearwright synthesis generated by `tools/audio/machine_sound_assets.py`.
No third-party or vanilla recordings were used in those compositions. Each
constant bed contains four minutes of evolving content. The generator and
its output manifest record the source method, duration, format, and checksums.
Moving mechanisms use four variations of damped material contacts; air uses
evolving pressure breaths, with no sampled recordings in either process.
Material contacts include surface grain, short rebounds and frame settling.
Liquid sources combine changing flow phrases, small splashes and collapsing
cavities; quieter wood, brass, iron and leather detail distinguishes bearings
and loaded motion. All of these layers are synthesized from scratch.
These original assets are covered by Gearwright's Apache License 2.0.

### Irrigation compositions using installed game audio

`machines/water-sprinkler.ogg` and `machines/water-irrigator.ogg` combine
filtered excerpts from Vintage Story's installed recordings:

- `survival:sounds/effect/watering-loop.ogg`
- `survival:sounds/effect/water-pour.ogg`

These are Vintage Story assets by Anego Studios and its contributors, not
original Gearwright recordings; Gearwright's Apache License does not replace
their original rights. The generator uses changing excerpts, overlap lengths
and impact groupings to compose four minutes per irrigation sound. Irrigator
watering excerpts overlap continuously, with a separately leveled, sparse
pour layer. Input
hashes and logical references are retained in the audio manifest. Unmodified
source files remain in the game installation and are not included separately.

## Retained third-party assets

The following files remain under their existing public asset codes for
compatibility and attribution. Current machine playback does not select them.
Do not use them as inputs to new audio work.

The retained hydraulic sound effects in this directory were sourced from
[Pixabay Sound Effects](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/) under the
[Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/).

Water recordings by **freesound_community**:

- “Dribbling Water,” Pixabay content ID 43910 (`water-dribble.ogg`)
- “Water Hose,” Pixabay content ID 103476 (`water-hose.ogg`)
- “Sprinkler,” Pixabay content ID 76541 (`sprinkler.ogg`)
- “Watering,” Pixabay content ID 62546 (`watering.ogg`)

Creaking recordings by **DRAGON-STUDIO**:

- “Heavy Wooden Creaking SFX,” Pixabay content ID 515253 (`pressure-creak-heavy.ogg`)
- “Abandoned Facility Creaking,” Pixabay content ID 463197 (`pressure-creak-facility.ogg`)
- “Creaking,” Pixabay content ID 401724 (`pressure-creak.ogg`)

Gearwright converts these recordings to mono Ogg Vorbis, normalizes their
levels, trims unused edges, and crossfades the water recordings for looping.
The source creators retain their rights in the recordings; Gearwright's Apache
License 2.0 does not replace the Pixabay Content License for these files.
